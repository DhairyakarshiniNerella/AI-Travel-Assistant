GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Goa is a popular coastal destination in India, famous for beaches, Portuguese-influenced architecture, seafood, and nightlife.

Top attractions:
1. Baga Beach - Popular for beach activities and restaurants.
2. Calangute Beach - One of the most well-known beaches in North Goa.
3. Fort Aguada - A historic Portuguese fort overlooking the Arabian Sea.
4. Basilica of Bom Jesus - A famous historic church in Old Goa.

Popular food:
- Goan Fish Curry
- Prawn Balchao
- Bebinca
- Poi bread

Best time to visit:
November to February is popular because of the pleasant weather.

Travel tip:
Carry sunscreen, comfortable footwear, and light clothes when exploring Goa.
