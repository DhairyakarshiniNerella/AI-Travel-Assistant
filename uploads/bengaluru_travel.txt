BENGALURU TRAVEL GUIDE

Bengaluru, also called Bangalore, is the capital of Karnataka and is known as India's technology hub.

Top attractions:
1. Bangalore Palace - A historic palace with Tudor-style architecture.
2. Lalbagh Botanical Garden - A large botanical garden with many plants and trees.
3. Cubbon Park - A popular green space in the city.
4. Vidhana Soudha - An important government building with impressive architecture.

Popular food:
- Masala Dosa
- Idli
- Vada
- Filter Coffee

Best time to visit:
October to February is generally pleasant for sightseeing.

Travel tip:
Use public transport or metro services when possible because city traffic can be heavy.
