TRAVEL GUIDE – INDIA

1. GOA

Goa is a popular tourist destination in India, famous for beaches, nightlife, Portuguese architecture, and seafood.

Best Time to Visit:
November to February is the best time to visit Goa because the weather is pleasant.

Popular Places:
- Baga Beach
- Calangute Beach
- Anjuna Beach
- Fort Aguada
- Basilica of Bom Jesus
- Dudhsagar Waterfalls

Activities:
- Beach activities
- Water sports
- Scuba diving
- Visiting forts
- Exploring local markets

Food:
Popular foods include Goan fish curry, prawn curry, vindaloo, and bebinca.

Approximate Budget:
Budget travelers may spend around ₹1,500–₹3,000 per day.
Mid-range travelers may spend around ₹3,000–₹7,000 per day.

--------------------------------------------------

2. HYDERABAD

Hyderabad is known for its historical monuments, rich culture, and famous food.

Best Time to Visit:
October to February is generally a comfortable time to visit Hyderabad.

Popular Places:
- Charminar
- Golconda Fort
- Hussain Sagar Lake
- Salar Jung Museum
- Chowmahalla Palace
- Qutb Shahi Tombs

Activities:
- Visit historical monuments
- Explore museums
- Try local food
- Shopping in local markets

Food:
Popular foods include Hyderabadi Biryani, Haleem, Double Ka Meetha, and Irani Chai.

Approximate Budget:
Budget travelers may spend around ₹1,000–₹2,500 per day.
Mid-range travelers may spend around ₹2,500–₹5,000 per day.

--------------------------------------------------

3. MUNNAR

Munnar is a hill station in Kerala famous for tea plantations, mountains, waterfalls, and pleasant weather.

Best Time to Visit:
September to May is generally a good period to visit Munnar.

Popular Places:
- Munnar Tea Gardens
- Eravikulam National Park
- Mattupetty Dam
- Echo Point
- Top Station
- Attukad Waterfalls

Activities:
- Tea plantation tours
- Trekking
- Nature photography
- Visiting waterfalls
- Wildlife sightseeing

Food:
Popular foods include Kerala-style meals, appam, puttu, kadala curry, and Kerala parotta.

Approximate Budget:
Budget travelers may spend around ₹1,500–₹3,000 per day.
Mid-range travelers may spend around ₹3,000–₹6,000 per day.

--------------------------------------------------

4. JAIPUR

Jaipur, known as the Pink City, is famous for forts, palaces, handicrafts, and Rajasthani culture.

Best Time to Visit:
October to March is generally the best time to visit Jaipur.

Popular Places:
- Amber Fort
- City Palace
- Hawa Mahal
- Jantar Mantar
- Jal Mahal
- Nahargarh Fort

Activities:
- Fort tours
- Cultural experiences
- Shopping for handicrafts
- Local food tours
- Photography

Food:
Popular foods include Dal Baati Churma, Ghevar, Pyaaz Kachori, and Laal Maas.

Approximate Budget:
Budget travelers may spend around ₹1,200–₹2,500 per day.
Mid-range travelers may spend around ₹2,500–₹6,000 per day.

--------------------------------------------------

5. MANALI

Manali is a popular mountain destination in Himachal Pradesh, known for snow-covered mountains, rivers, adventure activities, and scenic landscapes.

Best Time to Visit:
March to June is good for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.
December to February is popular for snow experiences.

Popular Places:
- Solang Valley
- Rohtang Pass
- Hadimba Temple
- Old Manali
- Mall Road
- Vashisht Hot Springs

Activities:
- Trekking
- Paragliding
- River rafting
- Snow activities
- Mountain sightseeing

Food:
Popular foods include momos, thukpa, siddu, and North Indian dishes.

Approximate Budget:
Budget travelers may spend around ₹1,500–₹3,000 per day.
Mid-range travelers may spend around ₹3,000–₹7,000 per day.

--------------------------------------------------

TRAVEL TIPS

1. Carry a valid identity document while traveling.
2. Check weather conditions before starting your trip.
3. Keep emergency contact numbers available.
4. Carry basic medicines and a first-aid kit.
5. Keep digital and physical copies of important travel documents.
6. Book hotels and transportation in advance during peak seasons.
7. Respect local culture and traditions.
8. Keep emergency cash available.
9. Avoid littering and protect tourist locations.
10. Check local transportation availability before visiting remote places.
